# Storylines: a film of "Who inherits the world?"

The one thing to remember: *simple rules fight it out on one board, nobody scripts the winner, and you can rig the game.*
Every board in the film is the real engine (`src/evolution.ts`) run deterministically from a fixed seed, and every screen is the real app (Tabletop design). Assumed for all three: 30–40 s, 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps, music plus sound on visible actions, no voiceover.

## A · One bad apple (recommended: a story a stranger follows, with a villain, a comeback and a twist)

**Device:** one real game told as a story. **Form:** one continuous take; the camera never cuts, it travels across the board and into the app's panels.
A trusting board of smiling cooperators. One spiky defector lands and spreads ring by ring; the cooperators around it dim. Then the eared Tit for Tat pieces push back, and the score track narrates each turn of the game in its own words. Then a hand takes over: paints a storm, designs a seventh piece, and opens Kaleidoscope, which blooms into the film's biggest picture.

- **First two seconds:** a close-up of the white board full of yellow pieces, blinking. A red spike drops into the centre with a wooden clack; its neighbours' smiles fade.
- **Beats:** defector lands → defectors spread (tension) → pull back to the box lid: "Who inherits the world?" → Tit for Tat fights back; moments pins drop on the score track ("Always Defect takes the lead", "… dies out") → a hand paints a storm and designs a piece → Kaleidoscope blooms (payoff) → "No winner is scripted" → end card on the lid.
- **Look:** the product's own Tabletop world: cobalt lid, white board, yellow actions, Lilita One over Rethink Sans.
- **Sound:** light wooden percussion bed; a clack per placed piece, a chime per moment pin, soft rain over the storm.

## B · Meet the cast

**Device:** the pieces are the characters. **Form:** object-to-object chain: each piece hops off the shelf into its own rule card, and the card flips into the next.
A heist-movie roll call for six game pieces: the trusting one, the spike, the one with ears that copies you, the visor that never forgives, the halo that forgives, the spinner that switches. Each gets a two-second intro built from its real rule card. Then they all drop onto one board together, and a seventh, star-topped piece arrives: yours.

- **First two seconds:** the six pieces on the shelf blink in unison; the spike turns and glares at camera.
- **Beats:** shelf roll call → six rule-card intros (one verb each: trusts, takes, copies, holds a grudge, forgives, switches) → all six land on the board and it starts moving → the designer builds the seventh → "Who inherits the world?" → end card.
- **Look:** the box lid and rule cards, each intro tinted with that piece's colour.
- **Sound:** playful pizzicato; each tell has its own sound (ears perk, halo ding, spinner whirr).

## C · Kaleidoscope (the bold one)

**Device:** the result first. **Form:** an endless zoom.
Open on a full-frame symmetric pattern pulsing teal, red, yellow and green, with no explanation. Pull back: it is a board of tiny game pieces. Rewind to turn 0: one defector among cooperators, temptation 1.85. Dive into one piece, into its rule card, out into the rules deck, out into the whole app.

- **First two seconds:** the pattern fills the frame and blooms one ring outward on the beat.
- **Beats:** pattern blooms → pull back to the pieces → rewind to a single defector → the one-shot rule in one line → zoom through a piece into the app (rules deck, board, designer) → "No winner is scripted" → end card.
- **Look:** the Kaleidoscope's four colours on cobalt, the app's white cards as the zoom's destinations.
- **Sound:** ambient pulses building; one tick per turn, rising as the pattern grows.
