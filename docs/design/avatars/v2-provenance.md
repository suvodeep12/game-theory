# Production strategy avatars — v2

Created 1 November 2026 with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, editing the [first cast sheet](cast-v1.png), then applying one correction pass. The production asset was `public/avatars/strategies-v2.png`, 1536 × 1024 RGBA (removed on 24 November 2026 when the Tabletop pieces replaced it; recover it with `git show f6dabec:public/avatars/strategies-v2.png`). It was the first avatar asset integrated into the app. The earlier cast sheet remains historical context, not the current visual authority.

The user required nice/nasty visual families and defined nice as never defects first. Five rounded characters represent error-free nice rules; the top-middle angular berry-red character represents Always Defect. The old sharp Win-Stay, Lose-Shift zigzag became a rounded asymmetric double-lobe shape. The formal classification and score semantics are in [score presentation](../../score-presentation.md).

The PNG is a three-column/two-row atlas in strategy order: Always Cooperate, Always Defect, Tit for Tat; Grim Trigger, Generous Tit for Tat, Win-Stay, Lose-Shift. CSS selects one equal square cell and keeps text names visible. No programmatic image editing or manual cropping was used. Browser checks verify the six positions, transparent corner and actual transparent ring centre. Transparent pixels retain coloured RGB values in the file; previews that ignore alpha may show a glow. Actual browser rendering was inspected on the app ground and shows the cut-out characters.

The correction requested removal of apparent haze and opening of the ring. Alpha inspection established genuine transparency in the final file. Small rendered avatars are present in tracked screenshots, but instant recognition has not been verified with beginners or a formal recognition study. A production edit does not imply independent aesthetic approval.

## Exact initial edit prompt

Edit the supplied character cast into ONE production avatar SPRITE SHEET, not a poster or an app screen. Preserve a cohesive simple flat illustrated character style and recognizable identities where possible. Transparent background, no text, no labels, no cards, no frames, no ground shadows, no small duplicates, no decorations. Canvas landscape exactly 3:2 with an invisible uniform grid of 3 columns and 2 rows: six equal square cells. Each cell contains only ONE full-body avatar, centered horizontally and vertically, confined within the central 75% of its cell. Leave equal transparent padding all around every avatar. Keep similar perceived visual size across the six characters. This sheet will be cropped into six avatars by the application, so cells must stay separate with no overlap. Strict cell order: TOP LEFT Always Cooperate, TOP MIDDLE Always Defect, TOP RIGHT Tit for Tat, BOTTOM LEFT Grim Trigger, BOTTOM MIDDLE Generous Tit for Tat, BOTTOM RIGHT Win-Stay Lose-Shift. The user defines NICE as never defects first in error-free play. Thus five NICE avatars must visibly belong to a soft rounded family with calm open eyes and gentle small smiles: 1 apricot scalloped five-lobed flower/pebble silhouette; 3 blue wide bean silhouette with two tall rounded forked antennae; 4 lavender tall arch silhouette with a broad rounded horizontal brim, keep its outline distinct from all others and give it a calm face; 5 golden yellow OPEN RING silhouette with a very clear transparent central hole, eyes on the upper ring, two rounded feet; 6 coral rounded asymmetric DOUBLE-LOBE S silhouette with one lobe offset left and another offset right, no angular zigzag and no sharp corners, two rounded feet. Only TOP MIDDLE Always Defect is NASTY: replace the teal block with a dark berry-red squat broad BLOCK silhouette with stepped angular shoulders, sharp triangular side corners and visibly angular brows/eyes; make it plainly different from the five rounded nice characters while still belonging to the same illustration universe. No weapons, scary teeth, gore, realism, glossy 3D, gradients or details that vanish at 32px. Nice family should read from the rounded silhouette, nasty from the jagged/angular silhouette even in grayscale. All faces readable at small sizes. Only these six characters on genuine alpha transparency.

## Exact correction prompt

Correct only the production defects in this six-character sprite sheet. Keep the six character identities, colours, poses, face designs, and the exact 3-column 2-row cell order. Remove EVERY coloured glow, halo, haze, soft shadow, gradient and background pixel outside the six hard-edged character silhouettes. Outside each character must be absolutely clear alpha transparency, not translucent coloured fog and not a painted checkerboard. Every character fill must be opaque and flat. Open the golden ring's central hole so the centre is genuinely transparent, like a doughnut. Cut out the gaps between legs, around the brim, and between antennae to true alpha as well. Keep all six centered in their own equal square cells on a landscape 3:2 canvas. Five rounded gentle characters and one angular berry-red angry block in the top middle. No text, no labels, no border, no small duplicates. This is a clean UI sprite sheet, not atmospheric concept art.
